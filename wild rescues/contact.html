Wild Rescues is a registered nonprofit wildlife rescue and rehabilitation facility, located in Ocala, Florida. We help injured,sick,neglected, and orphaned wildlife. We help rehabillitate these animals and then release them back into the wild, if possible

Wild Rescues is a member of the National Wildlife Rehabilitators Association.

We help many animals, such as:

  • Raccoons
  • Squirrels
  • Fox
  • Birds
  • Horses
  • Deer
  • Pigs
  • Reptiles
